Why the moment is right for all three

Music is splitting in two. AI is swallowing the commodity layer — already 44% of new uploads to Deezer are fully AI-generated — while real human work becomes the scarce, premium thing a brand can stand next to.

Tap to see where value moves

And in July 2025, TikTok pushed brands off trending sounds toward pre-cleared, multi-platform licensing. Two windows, both measured in quarters. They're the reason all three pillars matter now, not someday.

Pillar I · Verified Human Music

Proven human, not just promised

As AI floods the bottom, every platform will start printing "human-made." The word turns to noise. The difference was never the claim — it's the proof.

HRMNY is humans-only and shows its work: a human-made attestation with teeth, an AudD fingerprint, C2PA Content Credentials, and a hard ban on AI-generated tracks. Anyone can say it. HRMNY can prove it.

Not first on-chain. Not first cross-platform. First built for a world where any song can be faked.

Pillar III · Value Pricing

Fair to the artist, fair to the brand

Price moves on two axes. Scope sets the floor — more platforms and uses cost more, exclusivity most of all. Momentum guides the suggested price upward, pitched to brands as early access: license tomorrow's hit before it's expensive.

The artist keeps 100% of their price; the only add-on is a transparent 20% buyer fee. HRMNY suggests; the artist decides. Move the controls.

+ The synthesis

Each pillar makes the others work

They aren't three features bolted together — they're load-bearing for each other. Tap them and see.

Select the pillars to see how they hold each other up. Without verified human music, there is no worth — and nothing the proof or the pricing is even about. Without invisible Web 2.5, the worth is just a claim — checkable by no one, and the premium evaporates. Without value pricing, artists stop supplying and brands stop buying — the catalog and the proof sit idle.

! The floor under all three

None of it matters until the transaction works

The gate: liquidity is the first competitor. Payout rails are the precondition. All three pillars are favorability, and favorability is worthless to a marketplace that can't yet run its core transaction. Stripe Connect being unbuilt is the gap between HRMNY and its one real differentiator.

So the order doesn't bend:

  1. Foundation — Connect, payout-ready supply, clearance integrity
  2. Prove the payout promise via managers
  3. Press the opening

The window is open now.
