The gap it closes

Quality music, at the speed brands move

Brands can't get distinctive, cleared music fast — it's stuck behind agencies, labels, and weeks of legal. Indie artists can't get sync-ready or get paid fairly. The middle is empty, and AI is flooding the bottom — already 44% of new uploads to Deezer are fully AI-generated. Real, cleared, human music is the scarce thing a brand can move on now.

And in July 2025, TikTok pushed brands off trending sounds toward pre-cleared, multi-platform licensing — exactly the kind HRMNY hands over in a click. The window is open now, not someday.

Pillar I · Invite-only & Curated

A roster brands can trust

HRMNY is invite-only — a hand-picked roster of distinctive, real artists. Like a label, but the artist owns everything and keeps 100%. Curation is the product: a brand doesn't wade through a million tracks, they trust the stamp.

Every track is humans-only and cleared at the source — clean rights, single owner, ready to license. The taste is the moat.

Not the biggest catalog. The one you can trust without listening to a thousand tracks first.

Pillar III · Licensed in One Click

The deal closes on the spot

A brand picks a track, picks a scope, and clears it instantly — license generated, rights cleared, paid, receipt in hand. No agency, no label, no week of emails. The close is the product; everyone else stops at the pitch.

Pricing is transparent and scope-based — broader use and exclusivity cost more. The artist keeps 100% of their price; the only add-on is a clear 20% buyer fee. Move the controls.

+ The synthesis

Each pillar makes the others work

They aren't three features bolted together — they're load-bearing for each other. Tap them and see.

Select the pillars to see how they hold each other up. Without curation, a brand is back to digging through a million tracks — the trust, and the speed, evaporate. Without sync-readiness, even a great track arrives as a raw wav — dead on arrival, and the artist still doesn't get paid. Without the one-click close, you're just a prettier catalog to share — the deal leaks to email and the fee with it.

And here's the design principle: the artist value stands on its own. Being sync-ready, having a portfolio you're proud to share, getting paid 100% — none of that depends on a single brand showing up. Brand demand doesn't make HRMNY work; it makes it soar.

→ Down the road

A community, then custom campaigns

Once brands are here at a premium, the roster becomes a community — and HRMNY runs songwriting camps that write bespoke, pre-cleared music to a brand's brief. The early loop quietly manufactures exactly what camps need: a loyal roster, real brand relationships, and data on what actually sells.

The order doesn't bend:

  1. Curated, sync-ready artists + one-click licensing — prove a brand will buy
  2. Scale the roster; become the home artists run their sync on
  3. Community → songwriting camps → custom campaigns
